Privacy Policy — Tempo Tracker

Effective Date: November 22, 2026

Tempo Tracker ("we", "our", or "the App") is developed by Oleksii Hrishchenko. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Information: When you register or sign in using Email/Password or Google Sign-In, we collect your email address, display name, and unique user ID (UID) via Firebase Authentication.
  • Workout & Fitness Data: We store the workouts, exercises, sets, weights, repetitions, custom exercises, and workout templates you create in Cloud Firestore so you can sync them across devices.
  • Diagnostics & Crash Logs: We use Firebase Crashlytics and Firebase App Check to collect anonymous crash reports, device model/OS version, and app integrity signals to maintain stability and prevent abuse.

2. AI-Powered Analytics (Google Gemini)

When you request an AI workout insight, your aggregated exercise history (weights, reps, and dates) is processed via Firebase AI Logic (Google Gemini) to generate training feedback. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

3. Third-Party Services

The App relies on Google Firebase services (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Crashlytics, App Check, and Firebase AI Logic), governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

4. Data Retention & Account Deletion

Your data is retained as long as your account remains active. You can permanently delete your account and all associated workout history, templates, and custom exercises at any time directly inside the App (Account Menu → Delete Account) or by following the instructions on our Account Deletion page.
