Account & Data Deletion — Tempo Tracker

Users of Tempo Tracker have full control over their personal data and can permanently delete their account at any time.

Option 1: Delete Directly in the App (Instant)

  1. Open the Tempo Tracker app and sign in to your account.
  2. Tap the Account / Profile icon in the top-right corner of the History screen.
  3. Select Delete Account and confirm by tapping Delete Forever.

Option 2: Request Deletion via Email

If you no longer have access to the app, send an email to hun2033.dev@gmail.com with the subject "Account Deletion Request — Tempo Tracker" from the email address registered with your account. We will permanently delete your account and data within 7 days.

What Data Is Deleted?

Deleting your account permanently and irreversibly removes:

  • Your authentication profile (email address and UID)
  • All logged workout sessions and exercise history
  • All saved workout templates and custom exercises
  • Cached AI workout insights

No personal workout data is retained after deletion.
